What the estimates assume

  • You spend each available bar. Energy refills by 1 every 10 minutes, to a maximum of 72 in 12 hours. A full bar stops further recovery.
  • Food and drink can restore up to 24 additional energy per UTC day, shared across all items. This allowance resets at 00:00 UTC. The planner assumes you own and can afford the selected amount every day.
  • Actual gains roll independently from −3% to +3% per energy. A batch rolls each unit separately; splitting a batch offers no extra practice or XP.
  • Values are energy-budget estimates, not promises about calendar dates. You start with a full bar, so a milestone requiring three daily bars can be reached on your third visit. Gaps, activity rewards, future balance changes, or reaching a stat cap affect timing.
  • Level 100 and stat 100 are separate caps. Reaching 100 in one stat does not make the other three 100. This is the first balance model, intended to be tuned after playtesting.
Show the calculations and precision

Stat gain = 0.0125 / stat^0.75 × (1 + 0.25 × T / (T + 10,000)) × (1 + equipment bonus / 100) × luck

T is the total energy you have spent training. Practice approaches a +25% benefit; a higher stat takes more work to improve. Luck is a separate 0.97–1.03 roll for each energy. The next unit uses the new stat and T.

XP for level L → L+1 = ceil(500 + 20 × (L−1) + 0.0008 × (L−1)^4)

Maximum health is 100 + 5 × (level−1). A level-up adds the health gained from the larger maximum; existing damage is preserved. Combat and damage activities are still future work.

Stat saves use integer billionths: 1.234567891 is stored as 1,234,567,891 units. Item bonuses use integer parts per million: +3.4572% is 34,572 ppm, exactly. The server rounds each applied stat gain to nine decimal places. The interface shows fewer digits for readability.
